How to play
  • Draw with the pencil: mouse, trackpad, touchscreen or stylus (pressure-sensitive).
  • Zoom: scroll wheel, pinch, or the +/− buttons. Pan: right-drag, hold Space, two fingers, or the move tool.
  • Only the part inside the scored region (the dashed square) counts. Draw every branch.
  • Use the calculator, notepad, plotted points and asymptotes to work it out like you would in an exam.
  • Shortcuts: P pencil, E eraser, O point, H pan, F reset view, ⌘/Ctrl Z undo, Enter submit.
